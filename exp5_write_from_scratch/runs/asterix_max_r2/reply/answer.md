**Strategy.** The player never moves left or right; it stays in column 5, which is the middle of the board and its starting column. It only chooses between no-op, up and down. Every row holds at most one entity, so a row containing treasure has no enemy in it. Each step the policy scores three target rows: the current row (stay), the row above (up) and the row below (down). It then picks the best one.

- **Danger.** A row is deadly if an enemy is at column 5 or one cell away and moving toward it. An enemy two cells away and approaching is bad, and three cells away is slightly bad. A receding enemy is harmless.
- **Treasure.** A row with treasure approaching column 5 (or sitting on it) is attractive, and such rows are always safe.
- **Lookahead.** A move also counts 0.3 × the danger and 0.2 × the treasure of the row two steps away. This covers sticky actions repeating the move.
- **Edges.** Rows 1 and 8 get a small penalty because they have only one escape route.
- **Stay bias.** Staying gets a +6 logit bonus so the player does not jitter between equally safe rows.
- **Left, right, fire** get logit −60, so they are never chosen.

**Layer 1 (24 units used).** Each row has at most one entity, and its trail cell shows its direction. That makes direction readable from a single weighted sum per row.

- **Units 0–7, D_y = tanh(S − 2)** for rows y = 1..8.
  - S is the sum of enemy weights [0, .5, 1, 2.5, 5, 8.5, 5, 2.5, 1, .5] and trail weights [0, 0, −.5, −1, −2.5, −5, −2.5, −1, −.5, 0] across columns 0–9.
  - This gives S = 6 for an enemy at column 5, 4 / 2 / 1 for an approaching enemy at distance 1 / 2 / 3, and 0 for a receding enemy or an empty row.
- **Units 8–15, G_y = tanh(2.5·S_G − 2.5).**
  - S_G uses gold weights [1, 1.5, 3, 4, 6, 8, 6, 4, 3, 1.5] and trail weights [0, −1, −1.5, −3, −4, −6, −4, −3, −1.5, −1].
  - Approaching treasure maps to about +1, receding treasure to about −1, and enemy or empty rows to about −1.
- **Units 16–23, P_y:** a one-hot flag for "player is at (row y, column 5)".

**Layer 2 (24 units used).** These are gated value units, one per (player row y, action).

- Each computes tanh(−1.2·D_target + G_target + lookahead + 0.3 + edge penalty + 6·P_y − 6).
- When the player is not in row y, the gate pins the unit at −1, a constant.
- Units 0–7 are stay, 8–15 up, 16–23 down.

**Output.** Each logit is 25 × the sum of its 8 gated units, with biases cancelling the inactive −1 outputs.
