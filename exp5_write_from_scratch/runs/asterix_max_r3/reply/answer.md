## Design

**Strategy.** The player never moves left or right, so it stays in column 5, where every enemy and every treasure eventually passes. The only choice each step is up, down or stay. The policy is:
- Stay by default.
- Leave the current row early when an enemy is heading toward column 5 in it.
- Never enter an adjacent row with an enemy at column 5, or one cell away and approaching.
- Move into an adjacent row with treasure that is approaching or sitting at column 5.
- Stay put when treasure is approaching in the current row.
- Feel a weak pull toward treasure 2–3 rows away, and a weak push out of the edge rows 1 and 8.

**Layer 1 (32 units) = 8 blocks × 4 units, one block per player row y.** Each unit gets +20 from the player cells of row y and a bias of about −20. When the player is not in row y the unit sits at exactly −1, so only the player's block carries information. Within that block:
- **u0, u1, u2** look at row y, row y−1 and row y+1. All three use the same weights over the enemy, treasure and trail channels of their row. Since a row holds at most one entity, these weights can place every (position, direction) case at a fixed pre-activation level:
  - −3: treasure approaching or at column 5.
  - −4 or lower: treasure moving away.
  - −1.5: empty row, or an enemy moving away.
  - −0.5: approaching enemy 4–5 cells away.
  - 0.3: approaching enemy 3 cells away.
  - 1.0: approaching enemy 2 cells away.
  - 3.5: enemy 1 cell away and approaching, or at column 5.
  
  In row 1, u1 is a constant "deadly" signal (up does nothing there); in row 8, u2 is.
- **u3** is a signed far-treasure signal: treasure in rows y−2 and y−3 counts positive, treasure in rows y+2 and y+3 counts negative. It also carries the edge push.

**Layer 2.** Summing each unit type over the 8 blocks gives the active block's value minus 7, which layer 2 decodes:
- For each of stay/up/down: four steep threshold units mark danger levels L1, L1.5, L2 and L3. Two very steep units form a narrow band that detects "approaching treasure".
- Two threshold units read the far-treasure signal (up or down).

**Output logits.** Danger penalties are cumulative:

| Action | L1 | L1.5 | L2 | L3 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Stay | −3.5 | −7 | −11 | −22 |
| Move | −2 | −4 | −7 | −20 |

Bonuses and fixed values:
- Staying starts at +3, and gets +3.5 when treasure is approaching in the current row.
- Moving gets +4 toward approaching treasure in the adjacent row, and +1.5 toward far treasure.
- Left, right and fire are fixed at −60, so they are never chosen.
