## Design

**Strategy: intercept the ball where it will land, and keep a dead zone there.**

A ball at (x, y) moving horizontally h travels one column per step. Walls fold its path: the column sequence 8, 9, 9, 8 repeats with period 20. After the paddle moves on the frame where the ball sits in row 8 heading down, the ball is caught if the paddle is in either of two columns: X8 (under the ball) or X9 (the landing cell). These two columns form the target zone `{lo, hi}`. The paddle moves right if `pos < lo`, moves left if `pos > hi`, and does nothing inside the zone. The two-cell zone absorbs sticky-action overshoot.

The zone is `{fold(x+N), fold(x+N+1)}`, mirrored when the ball moves left:
- **Descending:** N = 8 − y.
- **Ascending:** N assumes the ball bounces off the next brick row, or off the ceiling from rows 0–1. By row, N = 9, 10, 7, 6, 5, 6, 7, 8.
- **Frame right after a paddle hit** (row 8, ascending): the target is column 9 − x. This works whether the hit kept or reversed the horizontal direction.

**Layer 1**
- **u8, H (horizontal direction):** uses dx' = dx + s(ball_x) + s(trail_x), where s = ±0.5 by column parity. This gives ±1 even when the ball slid along a wall (dx = 0) or on the reset frame.
- **u9, D (descending):** computes dy + 0.5 − [ball in row 8]. A flat frame (dy = 0) counts as descending, except in row 8, where it means the paddle just hit the ball.
- **u0–u7, comparisons:** one "move right" unit (`8·(lo(ball) − pos − 0.5)`) and one "move left" unit (`8·(pos − hi(ball) − 0.5)`) for each of the 4 cases (±h × descending/ascending). The ball's one-hot cell carries a lookup table of lo/hi values, and the paddle cell carries ∓column.

**Layer 2**
- **v0–v7:** AND gates, `tanh(4·(cmp + ±H + ±D − 2))`. Each fires only when its case matches the ball's current direction.

**Output**
- Right and left logits are ±10. All no-op actions have logit 0, so the policy is nearly deterministic.
